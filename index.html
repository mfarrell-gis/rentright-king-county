About this map: RentRight helps prospective renters evaluate King County census tracts based on personal priorities. Users can rank their preferred criteria by dragging rows and toggle factors on or off. Scores are normalized so the best available option always scores 100. The CartoDB Positron basemap was chosen for its clean, muted style that lets the choropleth colors stand out clearly. Data is fabricated for academic purposes, with transit and road buffer scores calculated from real Sound Transit station locations and highway geometry. Census tracts are the standard geographic unit for this type of socioeconomic analysis. Only active criteria are displayed, and only active criteria are weighted.

Data sources: RentRight's data is synthesized for educational use, drawing inspiration from real-world datasets such as the American Community Survey, EPA's Air Quality Index, and local crime reports. Transit access scores are derived from proximity to Sound Transit stations, while road buffer scores are calculated based on distance from major highways. The economic health score is a composite index inspired by local business density and growth metrics.

Criteria descriptions: Affordability considers the prevailing median rent price; Transit Access evaluates proximity to Link Light Rail stations; Safety is based on crime rates, both property and violent crimes alike; Air Quality uses mock EPA data; Median Income reflects local earnings; Away from Freeways and Golf Courses assess distance from these features; Walk/Bike Score measures pedestrian and cycling friendliness; Business Activity gauges local economic vibrancy; Proximity to Pins rewards areas near user-defined locations.

My inspiration: I really enjoy looking at what kinds of properties are available in different neighborhoods, if there are common building or architecture choices, and "vibes" of nearby neighborhoods. I also enjoy looking at how much is being asked for to rent or own in these kinds of neighborhoods. As someone who is interested in urbanism, there are things that I value that are not typically reflected on traditonal real estate website such as Zillow and Redfin, such as proximity to or distance from very specific locations, or air quality. I wanted to create a tool that would allow me to explore these factors in a more interactive way, and to see how different neighborhoods stack up against each other based on my own priorities. In life, a difficult thing to do is realize you don't know something, and find exactly where you can find that information you didn't know you needed, and that can be true of your knowledge of neighborhoods and cities as well. While this is just a proof of concept so far, I hope this tool can be a step towards that for people who are looking for a new place to live in King County, and want to explore the different factors that might influence their decision.
